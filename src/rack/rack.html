Compatibility & local report

DSP Cards and MIDI Room plugin files share one audio engine in this rack. Imported HTML is read for its DSP definition; its scripts are not run here. Mono MIDI follows each plugin’s declared frequency mapping. Unmapped generators play continuously while audio is enabled. Effects need an instrument in this rack.

Changes to the rack structure stop audio. All plugins stay local. Ordinary HTML instruments have their own audio engine; their sound cannot be patched into this DSP rack. A port of that engine is required first.
