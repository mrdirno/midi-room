Playing

Select a sound, then tap the pads or play MIDI notes 36–51. Assign changes voices and controller notes. Incoming MIDI keeps its velocity; touch uses the slider.

In MIDI Room, select a loaded TRITON in Sound to play it. Enable audio in that instrument first. Its current patch supplies the map. Local and external sound are exclusive. Focusing another card releases your held pads.

The local kit is synthesized percussion with hat choke, not sampled hardware. Rhythm players and shared clock follow remain independent.
