Playing & connections

Tap Enable audio once, then play the pads or your MIDI keyboard. In MIDI Room, use Wires to connect another instrument. Field Keys receives its notes, tempo, key and scale. A disconnected wire releases only its own notes.

Touch notes follow the current scale. Held notes keep their original pitch. A CV_SOURCE signal from 0–5 changes brightness. No recordings or data are uploaded.
