## Where this version came from

This repository begins with a reviewed publication snapshot on September 5, 2026. It does not manufacture an earlier Git history. The app was developed from Aldrin's existing Lucky Dreamer and TRITON browser instruments. Sanitized build inputs preserve the executable engine and musical attributions; private development manuscripts and prompts are excluded. Their source identities and transformations are recorded in the build and license documents.

Wish It Better's specification, queue design and contribution principles come from [mrdirno/vibe-cards](https://github.com/mrdirno/vibe-cards). Adopting that specification is a design lineage, not a claim that this entire app is a Git fork of Vibe Cards. The local instrument messaging interface also records its VibeBus examples in the component lineage.

The TRITON rhythm/harmony source names Groove MIDI Dataset (Google LLC, CC BY 4.0) and OpenScore String Quartets (CC0). These credits and the original executable data notices remain present. This publication has not independently matched every inherited transformed phrase to a newly downloaded corpus. TRITON Rack is an independent study, not a Korg product, endorsement or ROM distribution.

Faust, bundled DSP sources, and all other third-party notices retain their own authorship and license scopes. See the component inventory rather than treating the top-level license as a replacement for those terms.

## When you reuse it

Retain required license and copyright notices. Record the upstream repository and version, describe your change, and point users to the maintainer and wish route for your version. Do not overwrite the original creator's credit with the newest contributor's name. A contributor may choose a public byline or remain anonymous; that choice does not erase the project's inherited provenance.

Generated music is not automatically licensed by the software license. Do not put a software license badge on a user's recording or suggest that it proves their music's authorship. The RUN THIS GAME recordings, portraits and private prompts are separate from the app source license.
